About HeOCR

HeOCR reads Hebrew text out of photos, PDFs, Word files and images, on the phone itself. Free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.

Privacy

The app has no internet permission, and Android itself enforces that. Nothing you read leaves this phone, and the app collects nothing.

Accuracy

Text read by OCR may have mistakes. The app's accuracy has not been measured on a held-out set yet.
